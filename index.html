VYBE explores mood-led music discovery. This mini lab lets you shape the mood and energy of the concept.

Concept prototype: mood and energy change the visual direction only. No tracks are played or generated; this is not a finished music product.

01 / Signal

A photo can carry a feeling. Music discovery might start with that moment, not only with a genre search.

02 / Choice

Keep the mood editable. A person should guide the result instead of being boxed in by an automatic label.

03 / Next test

Explore whether mood plus energy helps people find a direction they like. This is a future test, not a completed study.
